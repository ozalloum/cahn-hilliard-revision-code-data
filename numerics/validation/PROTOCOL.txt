NUMERICAL VALIDATION CAMPAIGN

Purpose: replace previously acknowledged evidence gaps with reproducible
experiments. Negative comparisons and failed resolution checks are retained.
Original numerical results remain identified as legacy-grid comparisons.

1. Recover the 600-trajectory / 9,600-pair filter from documented seeds and
   save both coefficients and per-trajectory sufficient statistics. This is
   a regenerated fit, not recovery of the unavailable original array.
2. Independently test the original formula and a new Galerkin formulation
   with 2N cubic padding, zero Nyquist coordinate planes, normalized Fourier
   coefficients, and consistent padded-quadrature free energy. Verify the
   energy-gradient identity and manufactured cubic projection. ETDRK4 uses
   32-point upper-half-circle contour coefficients and fourth-order stages.
3. Space: N=64,128,256,512; one analytic periodic two-rectangle field and
   three shared random trigonometric polynomials. Times .0035,.035,.070.
   Time: ETDRK4 h=1e-4,5e-5; refine further if differences are material.
   Aim for a finest-grid difference below 1e-3 for field-level interpretation,
   and temporal differences below one tenth of the spatial difference or
   surrogate-comparator difference. These are empirical criteria, not bounds.
   Fourier-interpolate the smaller solution and compare on the larger grid,
   so omitted high modes contribute to the field error. Nyquist planes are
   zero in the Galerkin data. Aliasing comparisons use identical grid samples.
4. Original reference: 100/200/400 substeps at .0035 against independently
   refined ETDRK4 on the same collocation discretization. Spatial accuracy is
   a separate question. Never label the original 100-substep reference exact.
5. Re-evaluate all 100 short tests and the first ten through 700 steps with
   C1/C6/C12/C100, safe/unsafe residual, full-rank periodic shift-equivariant
   piDMD, and independent ETDRK4. Keep fields, per-trajectory results and
   safeguard logs. Treat 350/700 checkpoints as correlated observations.
6. Fit the published BCCB piDMD least-squares class on the same 9,600 input /
   target pairs. A(0)=1 preserves mean; no rank truncation or spectral-radius
   clipping. This is a reimplementation of that linear model class, not a
   neural-operator benchmark or claimed reproduction of a published CH test.
7. Stress: amplitude [.08,.3,.6,1,1.5], epsilon [.01,.02,.04], macro step
   [1e-6,1e-4,1e-3,1e-2], means 0 and 1, three random fields per mean = 360
   one-step cases. Retain nonfinite failures separately. Select a finite
   activating case for a clearly labeled diagnostic rollout, with a temporal
   reference. Also test a controlled overshoot and a finite 34-trial fallback.
8. New Galerkin filters on N=128,256: 60 independent training trajectories,
   16 states each, same cutoff 28 and ridge 1e-12, ETDRK4 teacher h=2.5e-5.
   The training budget is 960 pairs, not the original 9,600. Test ten shared
   new continuous fields at .0035; first three and rectangles through .070.
   Report frozen legacy-filter transfer separately from these new fits.
9. Cost: one common machine, five initial fields, five repetitions, warmup
   for each configuration, rotating method order. Include C1/6/12/24/100,
   safe residual, piDMD, fixed ETDRK4, and step-doubling adaptive semi-implicit
   and ETDRK4 methods at three tolerances. Report achieved error, wall-time
   dispersion, rejection counts and offline costs. No simultaneous campaign
   numerical process during cost.py; other desktop activity is uncontrolled.
10. Fit uncertainty: 200 bootstrap resamples of 600 whole training trajectories
    using stored sufficient statistics; fixed first ten test fields for
    conditional evaluation. Test uncertainty: separate paired trajectory
    bootstrap. Neither treats the 9,600 states as independent observations.
11. Sensitivities: cutoff [16,24,28,31], ridge [1e-12,1e-6,1,1000] on 20
    independent validation fields; retain all settings without retuning the
    nominal method using the test set. Shift ensembles: ten fields each for
    epsilon, mobility, amplitude, smoothing, mean and a joint displacement.
    Matched MT and matched M*macro_dt mobility runs are an exact time-rescaling
    control, not additional independent generalization evidence.

All arrays are float64/complex128. All new seeds, parameters and fields are
specified in the scripts and stored with results. New runs do not establish
unconditional stability, a fixed-34-trial positive-step guarantee, universal
generalization, or performance on nonperiodic/irregular/3D domains.

AMENDMENTS AFTER INITIAL PILOT RESULTS
The 256-to-512 late-time random0 difference exceeded 1e-3, so the stated
refinement rule triggers a further N=768 run. The 360-case stress sweep did
not activate at moderate amplitudes, so a separate exploratory 12-case
extension at mean 1, epsilon .02, amplitudes .08/.3, dt=1e-7/1e-8 and three
seeds is retained in its own CSV. A selected activating case illustrates
behavior outside the fixed-step training regime, not typical performance.
The real-FFT optimization is mathematically equivalent to the full-complex
Galerkin kernel and was verified before resuming saved checkpoints. Early
checkpoints used the full-complex kernel. Both are supplied. Campaign wall
times under concurrency are not used as comparative solver benchmarks.
Legacy radial-bin membership and the four axial cutoff-boundary exclusions
are explicitly matched to the original floating-point wavenumber formula.
All structure-factor metrics are refreshed from saved fields using these bins.
The final timing comparison omits campaign-only per-step logging and extra
post-mean diagnostic energy evaluation; it includes the original online
candidate energy tests, backtracking, mean corrections and any fallback.
Reference QA exposed amplified conjugate-symmetry roundoff in a long-time
Fourier ETDRK4 state. The corrected reference Hermitian-averages after every
step, as required by its real-field representation. Real-FFT and full-complex
implementations agree to 2.43e-16 on a long rectangle regression test; step
halving gives 3.21e-9. All spatial references are recomputed, and independent
ETD reference fields/metrics in the existing comparator runs are replaced.
Pre-fix runs are excluded from scientific results and retained only in the
internal rejected_reference_run audit directory. Original solver/filter
outputs are unaffected by this reference-solver correction.

Fixed-step cost comparisons use the labeled ETDRK4 step and shorten only
the final step; both coefficient sets are warmed. Refinement runs use a
uniform subdivision of each checkpoint interval, at or below the stated h.
The 24-entry ETDRK4 coefficient cache can be evicted by adaptive runs;
any resulting reconstruction is included in the measured wall time.
